2 MINUTE BRIEFING

What Swiggy is trying to measure — and why it matters

You're about to investigate a real Swiggy case. Before you watch, here's the context an analyst at Swiggy would already know.

THE METRIC: DAU (Daily Active Users)

DAU means the number of unique users who opened the app on a given day. It's Swiggy's most-watched number — it tells them how many people the app is actually useful to today.

⚠️ Here's the catch: Engineering, Growth, and Finance teams each define "active" differently. Before any analysis, you have to know which DAU you're measuring.

THE COMPARISON: WoW (Week over Week)

WoW compares this week's number to last week's. A 12% WoW drop means today had 12% fewer users than the same day last week. At Swiggy's scale — ~2.4M daily users — that's roughly 300,000 people who didn't open the app.

A 12% drop on a random Tuesday is a big deal. But is it actually unusual? That's the first thing an analyst checks.

THE USERS: THREE TYPES

🆕

New users

First time ever opening the app. If these drop, it's a marketing or referral problem.

🔄

Returning users

Opened the app within the last 30 days. The most valuable group — they already know and like the product. If these drop, it's a re-engagement problem.

💤

Resurrected users

Hadn't opened in 30+ days, but came back. Usually means a campaign or notification worked.

WHY NOTIFICATIONS MATTER

About 1 in 3 food delivery app opens comes from a push notification — "It's lunchtime, your favourite restaurant is open." Without notifications, returning users often simply forget to open the app.

This is why notification data is usually the first thing an analyst checks when returning user DAU drops suddenly.
