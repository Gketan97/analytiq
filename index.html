2 MINUTE BRIEFING

What Swiggy is measuring — and why it matters

THE METRIC: DAU

Unique users who opened the app on a given day. Engineering, Growth, and Finance each define "active" differently. Before any analysis, you need to know which DAU you're measuring.

THE COMPARISON: WoW

Week over Week. A 12% WoW drop at Swiggy's scale (~2.4M DAU) means ~300,000 people didn't open the app. A 12% drop on a random Tuesday is a big deal — but is it actually unusual? That's the first thing an analyst checks.

THREE USER TYPES

🆕

New users

First time ever. Drop = marketing problem.

🔄

Returning users

Active in last 30 days. Most valuable. Drop = re-engagement problem.

💤

Resurrected users

Inactive 30+ days, came back. Usually a campaign worked.
